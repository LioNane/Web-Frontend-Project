Monkey D. Luffy

Monkey D. Luffy, also known as "Straw Hat Luffy", is the founder and captain of the increasingly infamous and powerful Straw Hat Pirates. His lifelong dream is to become the Pirate King by finding the legendary treasure left behind by the late Gol D. Roger. He believes that being the Pirate King means having the most freedom in the world.
